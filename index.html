Who We Are

Not a club
that teaches.

DataForge is the technical data club at SRM IST Tiruchirappalli. We don't give certificates for showing up — we build operators who can clean, analyse, predict, and deploy end-to-end.

Everything we do is real work — real events, real datasets, real pressure. The Foundry Series is our proof.

Roadmap

The Journey.

From fundamentals to real-world deployment — seven milestones that forge a data operator.

Milestone 01
Data Science & Statistics Fundamentals
Probability, distributions, hypothesis testing. The mathematical bedrock every data decision rests on.
Milestone 02
Visualisation & Analytics Tool Mastery
Power BI, Tableau, Matplotlib, Plotly. Turn numbers into narratives that decision-makers can act on.
Milestone 03
Real-World Decision Making with Data
Business case studies, A/B testing, and data-backed strategy. From insight to recommendation.
Milestone 04
Industry Frameworks & Large-Scale Systems
Data warehousing, ETL pipelines, distributed processing. How organisations actually handle data at scale.
Milestone 05
Research & Industry Connect
Read papers. Talk to practitioners. Bridge academic knowledge and industry execution.
Milestone 06
Events & Datathons
Eight competitive events across four chapters. The theory meets the clock — prove what you know.
Milestone 07 · Final
Real-World Impact
Deployed solutions. Published insights. Measurable outcomes. The end of the pipeline — and the start of everything else.
